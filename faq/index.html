Which foundation is better for dry skin?

Hydra Glow Foundation is the first recommendation because it has a softer luminous finish and is designed for normal-to-dry skin.

Which foundation is better for oily or combination skin?

Velvet Matte Foundation is designed for a soft-matte finish and controlled shine.

Can purple eyeshadow work for everyday makeup?

Yes. Use lilac or mauve lightly. A small touch of champagne-gold can add warmth without making the look too strong.

What lipstick works with both palettes?

Rose Nude Lipstick is the easiest shared pairing for both the Soft Purple and Golden Mocha palettes.

What can I choose with a CAD $50 budget?

Any single product in the demo catalog is under CAD $50. The advisor can narrow the choice by asking whether complexion, eyes or lips are the priority.

How should undertone be used in recommendations?

Undertone should help match harmonious shades rather than suggest changing natural skin color. Warm/yellow-leaning undertones usually harmonize with warmer shades, while cool/pink-leaning undertones often suit cooler or neutral shades.

What if a customer asks for a product that does not exist?

The advisor should say that the product is not in the current LUNIVIA demo catalog and should not invent a product, price or feature.
