About the project

A fictional brand.
A real AI workflow.

LUNIVIA PARIS was created as a portfolio case study to demonstrate AI product content, visual consistency, product-data design and conversational shopping.

One project, multiple outputs.

Instead of building separate samples for every role, LUNIVIA acts as one connected project that can produce product imagery, AI video, website content, tutorials, social posts and an AI shopping assistant.

Workflow

01 Product concept and naming

02 AI product imagery

03 Structured product data source

04 Multi-page website

05 AI Beauty Advisor

06 Demo video and social content

Portfolio disclosure

LUNIVIA PARIS is fictional and created solely for portfolio demonstration.

No real purchases are processed. Product names, prices and formula concepts are illustrative.
